# Zeit AI event map: where the ICP actually is

**Date:** 15 Aug 2026
**Built from:** [icp-profiles.md](icp-profiles.md). Every event is scored against the ICP
segments, not against audience size.
**Status marks:** [verified] = date confirmed from an official or primary source ·
[confirm] = plausible but needs checking before budget is committed.

---

## The scoring rule

ICP density beats headcount. A 300-person association meeting where 80% of the room runs an
agricultural trading company is worth more than a 130,000-visitor fair where the ICP is
0.5% of the floor and buried in engineering.

**And one counterintuitive consequence.** The ICP has no data team. That is the whole reason
they buy an autonomous data engineer. So the AI and data conference circuit, which looks
like the obvious home for this product, is close to the worst possible spend: those rooms
are full of data engineers, analytics leads and competing vendors. The people who sign at
Zeit AI's customers are Geschäftsführer, MDs and Presidents, and they are at SAP user
groups, controlling congresses and industry association meetings instead.

## Tier 1: highest ICP density, book these first

### 1. DSAG Jahreskongress 2026
**6 to 8 October 2026 · Koelnmesse, Cologne · 5,500+ attendees · 180 partner exhibitors** [verified]

The single best match on the calendar. DSAG is the German-speaking SAP user group, so the
room is the ICP by definition: German, Austrian and Swiss companies running SAP, sending
controlling, finance, IT and management together. Zeit AI's core wedge is literally tracked
prompt #1, analysing SAP data in natural language without SQL. The 2026 motto even runs
against AI hype, which suits a proof-led pitch better than a vision-led one.

- **Segments:** A and B, plus the Head of Controlling champion.
- **Reality check:** this is roughly seven weeks out. Stand space and speaker slots are
  almost certainly gone. Treat 2026 as attend and prospect, then book 2027 immediately.
- **Verdict:** attend 2026. Exhibit and apply to speak in 2027. Highest priority on the list.

### 2. Congress der Controller (ICV) 2027
**26 to 27 April 2027 · The Westin Grand München, Arabellastr. 6 · 51st edition** [verified]
Fee €1,460 members / €1,690 non-members.

Europe's largest controlling gathering, and it is in Munich, which means near zero travel
cost and home-turf credibility. This targets the champion rather than the signer, which is
exactly right: the Head of Controlling feels the monthly Excel close and carries the pain
story internally.

- **Segments:** all three, via the champion persona.
- **Verdict:** speak if possible, attend regardless. Cheapest high-density option they have.

### 3. DER AGRARHANDEL association events
**August Börse 2026: 27 August 2026** · **Gut zu wissen im Agrarhandel: 23 September 2026** [verified]

This is the correction that matters for the agriculture vertical. DER AGRARHANDEL e.V.
(the 2022 merger of Bundesverband Agrarhandel and Verein der Getreidehändler der Hamburger
Börse) is the association of agricultural **trading** companies. That is Allgaier
Agrarhandel's actual peer group, and plausibly Raiffeisen's too.

- **Segment:** B, precisely.
- **Why this beats the big agri fairs:** see the note under Tier 2. Allgaier is a trader,
  not a farm.
- **Reality check:** August Börse is twelve days away. Too soon to do anything but attend.
- **Verdict:** join or attend as a guest, get Allgaier to make introductions. A named
  customer inside the association is the strongest possible entry.

## Tier 2: big fairs, real volume, thinner density

### 4. HANNOVER MESSE 2027
**5 to 8 April 2027 · Hannover exhibition grounds** [verified]

The world's leading industrial fair. Segment A and the manufacturing side of the customer
base are here, but so is everyone else, and the floor skews engineering and procurement
rather than finance and management.

- **Verdict:** do not buy a stand at current team size. Target a speaking slot in a
  digitalisation or AI forum, and run meetings booked in advance around the fair. The fair
  is the excuse to get executives in one city, not the channel itself.

### 5. EuroTier 2026 and Agritechnica 2027
**EuroTier: 10 to 13 November 2026 · Hannover** [verified]
**Agritechnica: 14 to 20 November 2027 · Hannover** [verified]

Both DLG-run, both world-leading, both **probably wrong for this ICP**. EuroTier is
livestock husbandry and Agritechnica is agricultural machinery. Their audiences are farmers
and equipment buyers. Zeit AI's agriculture customer is a trading and distribution business
whose pain is inventory, spend and margin across six locations, not tractors or animal
husbandry.

- **Verdict:** skip as an exhibitor. Worth one scouting visit to EuroTier 2026 only if the
  agri segment is confirmed as a real growth bet after the question in §12 of the ICP doc is
  answered. Do not let "we have an agriculture customer" pull the events budget to Hannover.

## Tier 3: brand and hiring, not pipeline

### 6. Bits & Pretzels 2026
**28 to 30 September 2026 · ICM Munich · 7,500 curated attendees** [verified]

Founders, investors and startup operators. Almost none of them are the ICP: the room does
not contain Geschäftsführer of €30M agricultural traders or Presidents of carved-out piston
manufacturers.

- **Verdict:** go, but book it against the right goal. This is a **hiring and investor**
  event, and the JD says hires still come from outbound too. It is also on Zeit AI's
  doorstep and the founders already have organic reach to convert there. Do not measure it
  on qualified meetings.

## The aviation problem

**EBACE is gone.** EBAA cancelled EBACE 2026 in April 2026, roughly two months before it was
due to run in Geneva, citing a format that "did not generate the momentum needed" and
competition from other European aviation events. [verified]

- **Replacement so far:** EBAA Week 2026, **16 to 18 November 2026, Skyhall, Brussels**,
  combining AIROPS with the Business Aviation Safety & Innovation Conference. [verified]
  Note the focus: operations, safety, ground handling. That is closer to Jetfly's world than
  a general expo was.
- **2027:** unconfirmed. A report suggests a successor at Cannes Mandelieu, but that is
  speculative and should not be budgeted against. [confirm]
- **Verdict:** aviation has no reliable European anchor event right now. Do not build the
  vertical strategy on one. If Jetfly is n=1, this is another reason to treat aviation as a
  case study to publish rather than a segment to staff.

## Segment A channel: private equity, not industry

Segment A does not gather at industry fairs. Its owners gather at PE events, and one PE
relationship opens a portfolio of companies with an identical trigger. Kolbenschmidt's owner,
Comitans Capital, is Munich-based.

- **MUPET (Munich Private Equity Training):** 17 June 2027, Munich [confirm]
- **German Private Equity Summit** (Informa / SuperReturn family) [confirm dates]
- **pemacom** M&A and private equity community events, Mittelstand transaction focus [confirm]

**Verdict:** this is the highest-leverage and least-developed channel on the whole map, but
it needs the §12 question answered first: is the Comitans relationship real, and has Zeit AI
ever sold into a second portfolio company? If yes, one PE conference beats every trade fair
on this list.

## Recommended posture given team size

One marketing hire at €60K to €120K cannot run a stand programme. The economics say:

1. **Speak, do not exhibit,** at the big fairs. A speaker slot costs negotiation rather than
   budget, and the JD explicitly makes negotiating speaker slots part of the job.
2. **Exhibit only where density is extreme and the room is small,** which on this list means
   the association events and eventually DSAG.
3. **Book DSAG 2027 now.** It is the one event where the entire room qualifies, and 2026 is
   already too late to do more than attend.
4. **Publish, do not just attend.** Every event produces an indexable, citable asset: a
   speaker page, a talk write-up, a customer story. That is the bridge between the events
   budget and the AI answer layer where Zeit AI currently sits at 0.0%.
