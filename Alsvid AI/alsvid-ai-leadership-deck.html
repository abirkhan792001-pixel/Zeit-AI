Zeit AI How · the levers

Four levers, each on a different constraint — so they add up.

S1 Partner channelStart now Distribution through the CargoWise and Timocom integrations we have already built — reach beyond the 213 without adding direct sales headcount.
S2 Pricing and ACVStart now Publish pricing, expand ACV against automation rate. More revenue per account, and we set the DACH price anchor before anyone else does.
S4 Road freight, via TimocomPilot A new mode on an integration we already run — a second market of comparable size to the beachhead, tested before it is funded.
S3 Self-serve tailGated to Q+4 Low-touch entry for the long tail of the 1,479-company ICP — volume the forward-deployed model cannot physically serve.

Reach, price, market, capacity — four distinct constraints. Dropping one does not cost a quarter of the result; it removes a constraint nothing else covers.
