Zeit AI 01 · Problem

European freight forwarding still runs on people doing the data work by hand.

What the work actually is

Quoting, track-and-trace, document handling, exception chasing. Every shipment is touched by a person moving information between systems that do not talk to each other.

Annual labour value of that work €3.4bn

Who has it, and why it persists

WHOFreight forwarders across DACH and Benelux · 213 in the beachhead, 1,479 in the full ICP
WHYThe substitute is headcount, not software · they add people when volume grows, because there is nothing to buy that does the work
SOCost scales with volume · the margin problem gets worse exactly when business gets better

We are not displacing a competing tool. We are displacing spreadsheets and hours — which is a harder sale to start and a much larger one to finish.

The status quo is manual, and it is expensive01 / 08
Zeit AI 02 · Solution

Agents that do the work, inside the systems they already run.

01 Connect, don't migrate CargoWise and Timocom integrations are already shipped. The forwarder keeps their TMS, their data and their process — nothing to rip out, which removes the objection that kills most deals in this segment.
02 Agents complete the task Not a dashboard that reports on the work. The agent quotes, tracks, reconciles and escalates — the human handles the exceptions the agent flags.
03 Automation rate is the meter The share of work completed without a human is both the product metric and the pricing metric. Price ramps as automation rises, so what the customer pays tracks what they stop doing.

The third point is the commercial design, not a detail: it aligns our revenue with their saved hours, and it is why expansion does not require a new sale.

Zeit AI 03 · Market size

The market is not the constraint. Our reach into it is.

Big category, narrow aperture.

Reachable today

Manual labour value

At today's ICP and pricing we can reach 3.4% of the value of the work being done by hand — a 29× gap, measured as labour displaced rather than as a TAM estimate.

And the beachhead alone cannot pay for the company.

Break-even is €2.92m ARR. Inside the 213-company beachhead that means ~30% penetration — roughly 64 accounts. Three of every ten forwarders in DACH and Benelux, to arrive at exactly zero.

Which is the real finding: the ceiling is set by who we sell to and what we charge, not by how much work is out there.

Zeit AI 06 · ICP and go-to-market

We can name every buyer — the integration list is the target list.

Who they are, and how we find them

WHOFreight forwarders, DACH and Benelux · 213 beachhead, 1,479 full ICP
HOWBy what they already run · a forwarder on CargoWise or Timocom is enumerable through those ecosystems. The integration is both the qualifier and the route in.
WHEREPartner channel first · then three events with pre-booked meetings, then direct into the named beachhead

Why the channel is not optional

Hard capacity guardrail 1 FDE ≈ 10 accounts

Direct growth is capped by how fast we can hire forward-deployed engineers. Reaching 64 accounts on the direct motion alone means carrying six to seven of them — the cost base grows as fast as the revenue does.

Partner-sourced and self-serve accounts are the only two paths that add customers without adding that ratio.

Identification is genuinely solved here, which is rarer than it sounds: we are not buying lists or guessing at fit. We know the names.
