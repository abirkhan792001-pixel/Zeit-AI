5U AI leads on product. It cannot yet be bought.

Three findings, one plan. Go-to-market work is all that stands between today and a €13-18m beachhead.

The constraint is distribution, not product.
In a 27-vendor grid, 5U AI clears 8 of 9 capability tests. No rival clears more than 6. Certified partner listings, published prices, referenceable customers and booked events are all missing.

Owner-managed Mittelstand, one decision-maker per account, worth ~€13-18m a year at full capture. Reachable through six city clusters, two partner rails (CargoWise and Timocom) and four industry rooms inside eight months.

Certify on CargoWise and Timocom. Publish a price. Land three to five references. Every move is costed on its own slide. EU proof is the gating asset: it sequences entry into the Nordics, then the US.

No rival publishes an enterprise price. The first to do it sets the anchor for the rest.
The case

02~€4,800 of FDE time ships unbilled with every added Worker. A ~€4,500 per-Worker deployment fee captures it. My estimate, open to correction.
031 public reference is findable today, in a market that buys on peer proof. Two or three visible ones change the conversion rate.
048x site visitors and 1,000+ LLM citations for one SCAILE client, with zero ad spend. Methodology on request. Here the same playbook targets pipeline, not traffic.
How I would run it
1 · Set the anchor. Weeks 1-2. A publishable price list on your desk, anchored to a €62k ops salary, risks mapped. The call stays yours.
2 · Line up proof. Weeks 2-4. Reference offer ready for sign-off, 2-3 reference conversations open.
3 · Get found. Weeks 3-8. Comparison and grounding pages live, built for LLMs as well as Google.
