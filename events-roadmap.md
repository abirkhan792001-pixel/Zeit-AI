---

## The four categories

Not every event is a sales channel, and treating them all as one is how events budgets get
wasted. Each event below carries one primary purpose.

| Code | Purpose | Success measure |
|---|---|---|
| **SELL** | Qualified meetings and pipeline | Meetings booked with a signer or champion |
| **PARTNER** | Channel and referral relationships | Introductions into a portfolio or member base |
| **TRUST** | Visibility, proof, being citable | Published, indexable assets and named references |
| **TALENT** | Hiring and investor relations | Candidates in process |

A note on TRUST: it is not a soft category here. Zeit AI sits at 0.0% mention across 102 AI
answers. Every speaker page, talk write-up and customer story is an indexable asset that
feeds the answer layer. TRUST events are the bridge between the events budget and the
AI-visibility problem.

---

## The budget reality, stated up front

Every ticket on this roadmap, for one person, across 18 months, totals roughly **€4,000 to
€6,000**. A single modest stand at Hannover Messe would cost more than that entire list
combined. Tickets are not the constraint. Time, travel and preparation are.

That is the argument for the posture throughout: **speak and attend, do not exhibit**, until
there is a second marketing person.

## Phase 1: Aug to Oct 2026. Attend and learn

The lead times are already blown on everything in this window. No stands, no speaker slots.
Go anyway, because the intelligence and the relationships are what unlock 2027.

### DER AGRARHANDEL August Börse
**27 Aug 2026 · Hamburg (confirm venue) · small association event · price not published**
**Category: PARTNER** (secondary SELL)

The association of agricultural trading companies, which is Allgaier's direct peer group.
Small room, extremely high density.

- **Lead with:** inventory management and procurement spend. Thin commodity margins across
  many SKUs and sites is the shared pain in this room. The credible hook is the published
  claim of 90 unprofitable products uncovered in one month.
- **Do not lead with:** the controlling cockpit. These are traders, not group finance.
- **Ask:** whether Allgaier will make introductions on the day.

### Allgaier Agrarhandel field days (Maisfeldtage)
**Late Aug to Sep, annual · Ebersbach and Pappelau · free, customer hosted**
**Category: TRUST**

A named customer hosts these. 2025 editions ran 29 Aug and 10 Sep; 2026 dates are not yet
published.

- **Lead with:** nothing. This is not a pitch venue. The play is a customer telling their own
  story, filmed, written up and published.
- **Why it matters:** this is the cheapest, warmest proof asset available, and it produces
  exactly the kind of specific, quotable content that AI answers cite.

### Bits & Pretzels 2026
**28 to 30 Sep 2026 · ICM Munich · 7,500 attendees including 1,500+ VCs, 300+ LPs and 600+ senior executives · €399 early / €499 startup ticket, students €199**
**Category: TALENT** (secondary PARTNER)

- **Lead with:** the company, not the product. The JD says hires still come from outbound,
  and this is the largest concentration of hireable operators in Munich all year.
- **Do not measure on:** qualified meetings. Almost nobody here is the ICP.
- **Worth noting:** the 600+ senior executives and the LP base make this a thin PARTNER play
  as a secondary goal, but do not let that justify the trip on its own.

### DSAG Jahreskongress 2026
**6 to 8 Oct 2026 · Koelnmesse, Cologne · 5,500+ attendees, 180 partner exhibitors**
**Tickets: members €990 for 3 days / €650 single day. Non-members €1,590 / €850. Students €250 / €100. All plus 19% VAT.**
**Category: SELL** (secondary TRUST)

The highest ICP density on the entire calendar. The room is German-speaking SAP customer
companies sending controlling, finance, IT and management together.

- **Lead with:** the controlling cockpit on SAP data. The wedge is exactly tracked prompt #1,
  analysing SAP data in natural language without SQL. Pair it with the trust stack, German
  hosting and no model training, because this audience will ask inside two minutes.
- **Second use case if the conversation opens up:** procurement spend cube built from SAP
  purchasing data.
- **Action now:** check DSAG membership cost. Member pricing saves €600 per three-day pass,
  and membership carries standing in the community that a vendor badge does not.
- **The real goal for 2026:** walk the partner exhibition, map who is already selling into
  this room, and book the 2027 exhibitor and speaker application before leaving Cologne.

## Phase 2: Nov 2026 to Mar 2027. Build and book

A quiet events window. Use it to convert Phase 1 relationships and to prepare Phase 3 rather
than to travel.

### The Stern Stewart Institute Annual Summit
**Schloss Elmau, near Munich · about 250 CEOs and CFOs of major German companies · invitation only, dates not public**
**Category: PARTNER and TRUST**

The single highest-value room found anywhere in this research, and it is not on any public
events list. Stern Stewart is both a Zeit AI customer and a shareholder through Stern Stewart
Ventures, so the ask is warm.

- **Lead with:** economics, not product. This room does not want a dashboard demo. It wants
  the argument: 370 hours a month recovered, €24M in improved negotiations, and the data team
  you cannot hire. Frame it as an operating-model change.
- **Action:** ask Stern Stewart for an invitation or a speaking slot. This is a conversation,
  not a purchase.

### Priorities in this window
1. Secure the DSAG 2027 exhibitor and speaker slot.
2. Submit the Congress der Controller 2027 speaking proposal.
3. Publish the Allgaier field-day story and the Jetfly case study.
4. Answer the Comitans question before any PE spend is committed.

### EBAA Week 2026 (optional)
**16 to 18 Nov 2026 · Skyhall, Brussels · registration fees not yet published**
**Category: TRUST**

Replaces the cancelled EBACE. Operations focused, which is closer to Jetfly's world than the
old expo was.

- **Blocker:** Zeit AI has **no published aviation use case**. Going without one means
  showing up with nothing to say. Build a fleet-utilisation and cost-allocation story from
  the Jetfly engagement first, or skip the event.

## Phase 3: Apr to Jun 2027. The first properly planned season

### HANNOVER MESSE 2027
**5 to 8 April 2027 · Hannover · very large · day ticket €35 advance / €55 at door, full run €90 advance / €108 at door**
**Category: TRUST** (secondary SELL)

- **Posture:** speak, do not exhibit. Target a digitalisation or AI forum slot, then book
  executive meetings around the fair. The fair is the reason executives are in one city, not
  the channel itself.
- **Lead with:** inventory management and procurement spend at plant level. This floor is
  industrial and operational, not group finance.

### 51. Congress der Controller (ICV)
**26 to 27 April 2027 · The Westin Grand München, Arabellastr. 6 · largest controlling gathering in Europe · €1,460 members / €1,690 non-members**
**Category: SELL** (secondary TRUST)

In Munich, so near zero travel cost. Targets the champion rather than the signer.

- **Lead with:** the controlling cockpit, and nothing else. Plan-Ist-Abweichung, automatic
  deviation alerts, drill-down from headline EBIT to purchase-order level. This room lives
  the monthly close. Diluting the message with inventory or procurement weakens it.
- **Posture:** apply to speak. A controller who hears a peer describe their close being
  automated is worth more than any booth.

### MUPET (Munich Private Equity Training)
**17 June 2027 · Munich · fees not published** [confirm]
**Category: PARTNER**

- **Lead with:** carve-out reporting. A divested unit loses group BI on a TSA clock and owes
  a PE owner board-grade monthly reporting with no team. That is the Kolbenschmidt story and
  it is the sharpest trigger in the portfolio.
- **Gate:** do not fund this until the Comitans question is answered. If Zeit AI has never
  sold into a second portfolio company, this is an unproven hypothesis, not a channel.
- **Note:** carve-out reporting is **not currently a published use case**. It needs a page
  before it can be pitched from a stage.

## Two gaps this exercise exposed

1. **Carve-out reporting is the sharpest trigger Zeit AI has and there is no page for it.**
   It is not among the three published use cases. It should be, before any PE event.
2. **Aviation has a customer but no use case.** Jetfly is a strong logo with nothing
   published behind it. That blocks EBAA Week and weakens the vertical story generally.

Both are content gaps, not event gaps, and both are cheap to fix. They also happen to be
exactly the kind of specific, entity-rich pages that get cited in AI answers.
